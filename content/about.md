My name is Martin Arias and I have dedicated a large amount of my lifetime
making sound happen. I always wanted to be able to create special spaces in
contrast of the real world and audio helped me through this mission.

I aim to produce work that is immersive in the world being built. No
distraction from that creative vision is acceptable.

> Technically, I start by researching the artist's or project's vision to
> identify how the audio can complement and enhance that world being built.
> Every technical decision serves that vision.
